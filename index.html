What it covers

Dashboard

Live totals and “things that need your attention”: overdue payments, low stock, pending wages, late work, cheques due.

Projects & work

Sites, work breakdown with progress and dependencies, pending work, site map, budget vs spent.

Clients & billing

Quotations with revisions, running (RA) bills with GST and retention, receipts, pending amounts, cheques.

Materials

Stock per site and main store, purchases, suppliers and price history, material requests, transfers.

Labour

Attendance grid, weekly wage sheet paid in one go, advances, labour contractors with work orders and bills.

Accounts

Money in and out, expenses, payables, bank and cash with running balances, reconciliation, site cash, month lock.

Site

Daily reports filled from the day's records, site issues, equipment, documents and photos, WhatsApp reminders.

Reports

Project profit, client outstanding, supplier dues, GST on bills, stock, attendance — print, PDF or CSV.

Safe by design

  • Permissions checked on the server for every action
  • Login locks for 15 minutes after 3 wrong tries
  • Optional 2-step login with an authenticator app
  • “Forgot password” code only to a confirmed email
  • Money records can’t be deleted — only reversed
  • Every change written to an audit log
  • One-click backup and tested restore
  • Import workers, materials and suppliers from Excel
